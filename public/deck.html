How it works today

Nonprofits pay a stream of small bills for people in crisis, and check each one by hand.

Each payment comes out of a grant, and each grant has its own rules about who qualifies, how much, and for what. A person checks every bill against all of it, then signs.

How it works

AI reads the paperwork. Code checks the rules. Two people decide.

01 · Claude
Reads

Turns a receipt or bill into who, how much and when, with a confidence for each. It never makes the call.

02 · Plain code, not AI
Checks

Grant rules, the bank, vendor identity, public records, duplicates. Same request, same answer, every time.

03 · Two different people
Decide

One certifies, another releases the money. Every step goes into a log that can't be quietly edited.

It gets smarter, safely

It learns from your staff, without switching off the checks that matter.

A supervisor approves one line of a family phone plan. Signet turns that into a rule, shows what it would change, and a second person switches it on. The other lines clear. A reused receipt still doesn't.
